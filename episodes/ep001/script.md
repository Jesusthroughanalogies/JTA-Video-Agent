## Overview (v2: AI and animation first)

A 12 to 14 minute video told almost entirely through AI video and 2D animation, narrated in your real voices. You appear on camera for under 20 seconds in total. Each part is an AV table: the words on the left, the shot on the right, so the agent can turn every row into a clip.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Title | Is There a God? 3 Pictures That Explain It |
| Alternatives | Does God Exist? The Best Arguments, Side by Side · Is There a God? Explained in 3 Pictures |
| Length | ~1,800 spoken words, 12 to 14 min |
| Voices | Matt (MATT) and Jack (JACK), real recorded voiceover |
| On camera | One 5-second intro clip and one 5-second outro clip |
| Look | Cinematic AI footage for the world and the story; clean 2D motion graphics for the three analogies. Everything on screen is first century or earlier, except your own photos and clips. Jesus is always the saved JTA-Jesus character |

**Shot tags** (each visual starts with one):

- **[AI]** cinematic AI video clip, 4 to 8 seconds, generated in Higgsfield
- **[ANIM]** 2D motion-graphics animation built in code (dominoes, dials, painting, diagrams)
- **[TEXT]** scripture or quote card over a background
- **[CLIP]** your short on-camera clip from the Drive
- **[PHOTO]** your real photos or footage from the Drive
- **[STOCK]** free stock footage, fallback only

Music and sound cues are marked *(music: …)* and *(sfx: …)*. A new shot roughly every 4 to 6 seconds keeps the pace up.

## What you drop into the Drive

Your total time for this video is about 90 minutes: two 5-second clips, one voiceover session and a photo pick. The agent does the rest.

**Recording the voiceover**

- Lav or USB mic, soft room, no fan or music.
- Read it like you're telling a friend, not reading. Small slips are fine.
- Pause a beat after each scripture reference.
- Format: WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono. Loudest peaks around -6 dB, never in the red.
- Start each file with 10 seconds of silence in the room, so the agent can clean the background noise.
- Recording in different places? Each of you records your own lines per part: 02_part1_matt.wav, 02_part1_jack.wav.
- Test first: upload 01_hook.wav on its own and tell the agent. It checks the sound and transcription before you record the rest.
- If you change a line while recording, that's fine: the agent transcribes the audio and follows what you said.

**Fact-check before recording**

- [ ] Fred Hoyle quote: confirm wording and source (commonly cited from his 1981 article in *Engineering and Science*).
- [ ] "Searched more than any other": based on US Google data reported by RELEVANT (April 2025).
- [ ] Age of the universe: about 13.8 billion years.
- [ ] Scripture is now NIV. Check each verse in the YouVersion app (NIV) before recording: Genesis 1:1, Psalm 19:1, Romans 1:20, Romans 2:15, John 1:1, John 1:14, Hebrews 1:1-2, Jeremiah 29:13. Bible sites are blocked from the agent's network, so the exact wording isn't confirmed yet.
- [ ] Fred Hoyle "didn't believe in God": his views on God shifted over his life. Consider "who wasn't religious" instead.
- [ ] Every scripture card shows "NIV" after the reference; the video description carries the NIV copyright line.
