You can't scrub this stain out. Picture a white shirt. One drop of red dye. You grab a cloth and scrub. It doesn't come out. It spreads. That's what we do with sin. We scrub harder. Be nicer. Go to church more. Try to cover it. But scrubbing doesn't remove a stain. It just moves it around. God never asked you to clean yourself up. Isaiah says, though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow. Not lighter. Not hidden. White. That's what Jesus does with the stain you can't get out.
