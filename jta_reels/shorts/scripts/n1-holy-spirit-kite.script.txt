Trying harder won't lift you. Ever tried to fly a kite with no wind? You run. You pull. You run faster. It lifts for a second, then crashes. A lot of us live the Christian life like that. Running. Pulling. Crashing. But a kite was never meant to fly by effort. It was made to catch the wind. Jesus said the Spirit is like the wind. You can't see it. You can't control it. But you can see what it lifts. The Holy Spirit isn't the reward for trying harder. He's the wind. Stop running. Let him lift you.
