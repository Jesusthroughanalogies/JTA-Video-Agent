One Greek word changes everything. Imagine a debt you could never pay. Every month the bill gets longer. Every mistake adds a line. You work harder, but the number only grows. On the cross, Jesus cried out, it is finished. John writes it as one Greek word. Tetelestai. In everyday Greek, it was used when a debt was paid. Paid in full. Not paid in part. Not paid if you behave. Paid. That's grace. You don't work off the bill. You receive the receipt.
