There's a gap no one can jump. Picture a canyon. You on one side. God on the other. Some people try to jump it. Good deeds. Religion. Being better than most. Some jump further than others. But nobody makes it across. The gap is too wide. So God didn't shout instructions from the other side. He built a bridge. Jesus said, I am the way. Not a way. Not one of many good tips. The way. The bridge isn't how far you can jump. It's who you trust to carry you across.
