# YouTube Compliance (check every video against this)

Sources: a third-party summary of YouTube's 2025 to 2026 policy changes (AIR Media-Tech, "YouTube Monetization Policy Changes 2026") and YouTube's advertiser-friendly guidelines. Re-check YouTube's own policy pages every quarter; rules change.

## Inauthentic content (the biggest risk for an AI-heavy channel)

YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" policy to "inauthentic content" in July 2025 and ran a demonetisation sweep in January 2026. Flagged channels typically showed synthetic narration, templated thumbnails, looped stock footage and an unsustainable upload pace.

JTA stays clearly authentic by:

- Real human voiceover by Matt and Jack (never a synthetic narrator for the main narration).
- Original scripts, original analogies and custom animation in every video.
- Real founders' photos, clips and personal stories in every video.
- Distinct structure, visuals and thumbnail per video. No templated repeats.
- A sustainable pace: about 1 to 2 long videos a week.

## AI disclosure

- Switch on the altered or synthetic content disclosure for every video with photorealistic AI footage.
- Since May 2026 YouTube auto-applies labels when it detects undisclosed photorealistic AI; non-disclosure can mean removal or suspension from the Partner Program.
- Disclosure does not reduce ads.

## Advertiser-friendly content

- Sensitive topics (suffering, death, the crucifixion) can earn full ads when handled non-graphically and educationally. Keep violence wide, distant, silhouetted.
- No gore, no sexualised imagery, no shocking imagery of young people in distress.
- Avoid inflammatory language about other religions or groups. Disagree respectfully.

## Audience setting

- Always "not made for kids". JTA long form is for teens and adults.

## Partner Program thresholds

- Current: 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days).
- Reported change: from 1 February 2027, new applicants need 8,000 watch hours. Confirm on YouTube's own pages; if true, prioritise applying before then.

## Copyright

- Scripture: NIV, within Biblica's 500-verse allowance. Show "NIV" on every scripture card and the NIV copyright line in every description (wording in `script-rules.md`).

- Music and sound: ElevenLabs-generated or logged royalty-free only.
- Stock: log the source and licence of every clip.
- Never use other creators' footage, film clips or copyrighted songs.
