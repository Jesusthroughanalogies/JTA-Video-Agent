# JTA Script Rules (long form)

## Audience and tone

- Written for someone with no church background. Every line must be clear to a 14 to 15 year old.
- Warm, honest, conversational. Two friends explaining something that changed their lives, not a sermon or a lecture.
- Never assume the viewer has seen another episode. Introduce every analogy fresh.
- Don't leave more questions than answers. Every video ends with the answer in Jesus and a simple next step.

## Structure (12 to 14 minutes, about 1,700 to 1,900 spoken words)

1. **Hook (0:00 to 1:00):** the question in the first 5 seconds, why it matters, who we are (one line), and the promise of the video (e.g. "three pictures").
2. **Parts 1 to 3:** one analogy per main argument. Each part: the picture, the argument in plain words, the scripture, the sceptic's strongest reply answered fairly, a one-line handover to the next part.
3. **Personal story (60 to 75 seconds):** Matt or Jack in their own words over their real photos. Provide prompts only; never write their experiences.
4. **The answer:** how the topic points to Jesus, with a practical next step for the viewer.
5. **Outro:** subscribe line, share line ("send this to someone who..."), and the teaser for the next topic.

## Format

- AV tables, one per part: `# | Words | Visual`.
- Speaker names in bold the first time they speak in a row: **MATT:** or **JACK:**. Split lines between them so neither talks for more than about 40 seconds straight.
- Every visual starts with a shot tag: [AI], [ANIM], [TEXT], [CLIP], [PHOTO], [STOCK]. Add music and sound cues in italics.
- Aim for one row per 4 to 8 seconds of speech.

## Accuracy

- Scripture in NIV (2011) wording, with the reference and "NIV" on screen as a [TEXT] card (e.g. "Psalm 19:1 (NIV)"). Copy the verse from a fetched NIV source, never from memory; if no source is reachable, flag the verse for the humans to check.
- NIV permission: up to 500 verses may be quoted without written permission (Biblica). Put the NIV copyright line in every video description: "Scripture quotations taken from The Holy Bible, New International Version® NIV®. Copyright © 1973, 1978, 1984, 2011 by Biblica, Inc.™ Used by permission. All rights reserved worldwide."
- Quote scholars and scientists only with a verifiable source. Put each one in the fact-check list.
- Present the strongest sceptic reply, in words a sceptic would agree with, before answering it.
- Stay within historic, mainstream Christian belief. Avoid fights between denominations.
- Word-level accuracy notes (like "hamartia" meaning "miss" in classical Greek usage but "sin" in the New Testament) must be stated precisely.

## Words

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Concrete pictures over abstract words.
- No jargon without an instant plain explanation.
- Avoid churchy clichés.
